# combating-money-laundering-bangladesh
Academic research on anti-money laundering laws, enforcement challenges and legal reforms in Bangladesh.
Combating Money Laundering in Bangladesh: Challenges, Legal Responses and the Way Forward

Author: Majharul Islam
Affiliation: Department of Law, Northern University Bangladesh
Research Area: Criminal Law, Financial Crime, Anti-Money Laundering and Legal Reform

Abstract

This research examines the legal and institutional framework for combating money laundering in Bangladesh. It explores key enforcement challenges, institutional coordination, financial investigations, asset recovery and international cooperation. The study also considers possible legal and policy reforms to strengthen the effectiveness of anti-money laundering efforts.

Research Objectives

- Examine the legal framework governing money laundering in Bangladesh.
- Assess institutional and enforcement challenges.
- Explore investigative, prosecutorial and asset-recovery mechanisms.
- Identify potential legal and policy reforms.
